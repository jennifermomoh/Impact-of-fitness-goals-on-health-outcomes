Study Questions:
1. Does setting fitness goals in Google Fit positively impact health outcomes?
2. How do user behaviours compare to their pre-goal baseline?

Study Goals:
1. To undererstand how goal setting impacts usrs health outcomes and why
2. To provide strong backing for the sales team to pitch product to customers, especially on the importance of using the 
goal setting features


Key Metrics:
Goals: calories burned, active minutes, step count, weight
Outcomes: weight, blood pressure, heart rate, sleep scores

Hypotheses:
1. Goal-setting increases motivation and adherence, leading to improved outcomes.
2. Users will show higher activity levels and consistency post-goal setting.


Methods:
- non experimental


What is important:
- Health Metrics
- Timelines
